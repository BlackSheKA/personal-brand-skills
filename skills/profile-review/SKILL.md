# Profile review

A profile is a sales page for a personal brand. Someone sees a good post, clicks the name, and in about five seconds decides whether this person can help them. Your job: make that page say one clear message to the right client and point to one next step.

## Before you start

- If `personal-brand.md` exists in the project, read it: the one sentence, the ideal client, the offer, the voice and the call to action are the yardstick.
- Ask the person to **paste the text of their profile** (headline or name line, bio or about section, featured or link section, current role) and say which platform: LinkedIn, Instagram or X in this light version. A screenshot also works if you can read images.
- If there is no brand definition, ask in one message: who their ideal client is, what they sell, and where people should go next.
- Write in the person's language.

## How to judge

Check each element against these questions:

1. **Clarity:** Does a stranger understand in five seconds who this is for and what result they get? Jargon and job titles alone ("Founder | Speaker | Visionary") fail.
2. **One message:** Do headline, bio and about section repeat the same promise, or say three different things?
3. **Proof:** Is there one concrete reason to believe (a number, a known client, years, a result)? Only real facts.
4. **Next step:** Is there one clear call to action and a working link (book a call, newsletter, website)? Several competing links fail.
5. **Voice:** Does it sound like the person, or like a template?
6. **Platform limits:** LinkedIn headline up to 220 characters, about section up to 2,600; Instagram bio up to 150 characters; X bio up to 160 characters.

## Output

1. **Score from 1 to 10** and one sentence on the biggest problem.
2. **The 5 most important fixes**, ordered by impact. For each:
   - the element (headline, bio, about, link...),
   - what is wrong, in one line,
   - **the new text, ready to paste**, within the platform limit,
   - why it will bring more of the right clients.
3. If a fix needs a fact you do not have, write it with a visible `[[placeholder]]` and ask.

Never invent numbers, clients or results. Respect words the person never uses and any rules of their profession (for regulated professions such as lawyers, doctors and financial advisors, avoid testimonials and result promises unless they confirm these are allowed).

## What this light version leaves out

Here you get the 5 biggest fixes for one profile from pasted text. A full review reads the profile from its link, marks every element on a screenshot, checks the photo and banner, covers every platform the person runs, and compares each element with a complete brand definition, so all profiles repeat one message.
