# Repurpose content

You turn one source piece into posts that feel native on each platform, not the same text pasted everywhere. One idea, many shapes.

## Before you start

- If `personal-brand.md` exists in the project, read it and follow the ideal client, the voice profile and the call to action.
- Ask for the source (paste text, a transcript, or notes) and **which platforms, up to 3**. If they do not know, suggest the 3 that fit their ideal client best and explain why in one line.
- Write in the person's content language.

## Step 1: find the ideas

Read the source and list the 3-7 separate ideas, stories, numbers or opinions inside it. Each piece you write uses **one idea**. Pick the strongest idea for each platform; do not cram the whole source into every post.

## Step 2: write native versions

Rules for every piece:
- Use only facts, stories and numbers from the source or the brand definition. Never invent results or quotes. Missing detail: visible `[[placeholder]]`.
- Plain, spoken language; one idea per line; specific beats vague.
- Opening line: clear first, curious second. A stranger must understand it.
- One call to action at the end, matching the content (follow or newsletter for insight, free resource for how-to, direct offer rarely). No invented links.
- Never beg for likes, no "Thoughts?", no hashtag spam.

Platform shapes:
- **LinkedIn post:** 1,300-1,700 characters. Hook, context, 5-7 points (bold complete thought plus an example), takeaway, CTA. No link in the text; add it in a comment after 30 minutes.
- **X post:** one idea, under 280 characters. Statement, contrarian line or short list. Write "X", not "Twitter". **X thread:** 5-8 posts, each under 280 characters, the first is hook plus promise, the CTA as a final "P.S." post.
- **Instagram caption:** the first line is the hook; one idea per line with blank lines between; CTA for "link in bio". Say what photo or video to use. **Carousel:** 7-10 slides as `--- Slide N ---`; slide 1 is the hook (15-25 words), one idea per middle slide, CTA on the last.
- **Threads:** under 500 characters, one idea, conversational.
- **Facebook:** 60-200 words, one idea, conversational, ends with a question or the CTA.
- **Short video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts):** 100-120 words, about 45-60 seconds. Beats on separate lines: hook in the first 3 seconds, problem, common mistake, better approach, example, benefit, one tip to try today, closing line. Add `[show: ...]` notes with a new visual every few seconds.
- **Newsletter:** "Subject:" under 40 characters and "Preview:" 35-50 characters, a short personal opening, one idea under 2-3 subheadings with steps and a real example, sign-off, P.S. with the link. About 600 words.

## Output

For each platform: the ready piece, then one line saying which idea from the source it uses. Finish with a suggested order and spacing (for example: LinkedIn Monday, short video Wednesday, X Friday).
